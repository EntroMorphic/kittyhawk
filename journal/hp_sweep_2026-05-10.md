Date: 2026-05-10. Per TD-20 (substrate quality degradation vs HF on
multi-step reasoning, code completion, structured output), re-swept the four
substrate hyperparameters that were originally tuned on the pre-RMSNorm-fix
substrate. The fix changed activation magnitudes flowing through the network
(post_attn_norm outputs went from magnitude-collapsed to ~6.5× larger), so
prior tuning was suspected drift candidate.

## Why does GATE_ACT_BX = 1 help?

The gate path is `gate_act = relu²(gate_proj_output) × up_proj_output`.
Both inputs are at `BITNET_FFN_BX = 6`; product is at `2 × FFN_BX = 12`,
narrowed to `GATE_ACT_BX` for the next normalization.

At GATE_ACT_BX = 2: divide by 3^(12-2) = 3^10 = 59049. The narrowing throws
away ~16 bits of dynamic range per cell. Most cells fit easily, but outliers
(the gate²·up products that would be largest in real-space) were getting
quantized into bins where adjacent cells become indistinguishable.

At GATE_ACT_BX = 1: divide by 3^(12-1) = 3^11 = 177147. The narrowing is
3× more aggressive, BUT the result lands at scale 3^1 instead of 3^2, which
gives 3× more real-space range (MAX_VAL/3^1 ≈ 194M vs MAX_VAL/3^2 ≈ 64.5M).

So GATE_ACT_BX = 1 trades 1 trit of fractional precision for 3× more
dynamic range in the FFN intermediate. The downstream `ffn_sub_norm`
normalizes magnitude away anyway, so the lost fractional precision matters
less than the wider range that prevents outlier cells from being clipped or
quantization-binned.

Why the original tuning chose 2: pre-RMSNorm-fix, the residual stream
magnitudes were 6.5× smaller (silent saturation collapse), so the FFN
gate²·up products were correspondingly smaller and the wider range of
GATE_ACT_BX = 1 was unnecessary. The original sweep saw "Pearson invariant
∈ [1, 6]" because the test workload didn't exercise the regime where the
range advantage matters. Post-fix, the residuals are at correct magnitude,
the gate²·up products span more of the available range, and GATE_ACT_BX = 1's
wider range becomes load-bearing.

## What this doesn't address

- **factual_hamlet regression** is real. Direct answer became a "Hint"
  prompt. Worth investigating if a different knob combination (e.g.,
  `gate1 + fudge2`) recovers it. Recorded as TD-22.
- **`math_div` still indirect** at gate1. The model gives "12 × 12 = 144"
  instead of "144 / 12 = 12". This is a model-capability quirk on a 2B
  parameter LLM under greedy decoding, not a substrate issue per se.
- **`fudge2` and `gate1` combination** untested. Each individually
  improves; combined could be either better or interfere. Out of scope
  for this cycle — the gate1 single-knob win is already substantial.
- **HF-vs-substrate confirmation** not re-run for the new config. Open
  TD on the broader question of substrate quality vs reference.

## Closure

TD-20 partially addressed: the substrate is now coherent on 4 of the 5
previously-failing prompts. Strict pass rate jumped from 63% to ~80% with
zero hard failures. Default updated to `BITNET_GATE_ACT_BX = 1`. TD-22
opened for the factual_hamlet regression and the gate1+fudge2 combination
to investigate.

Methodology lift: when a substrate metric or hyperparameter is tuned on
buggy state, retune after the fix. The empirical-tuning provenance of every
knob (date, journal, baseline at the time) becomes load-bearing once any
upstream change moves the activation distribution.
